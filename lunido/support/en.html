Common questions

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

My data doesn't show up on my other device.

Check that both devices use the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for Lunido in the iOS Settings app (your name › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

The list doesn't fit my country or region.

In Settings, tap Suggest a list correction and tell us what to change.

Is this medical advice?

No. Lunido is a shopping and packing list. Safety notes cite official sources; for health questions, talk to your doctor or midwife.

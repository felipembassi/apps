Lunido

Privacy policy · Android

Updated October 27, 2026.

Lunido for Android has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells your data. You don't need an account: your list stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What stays on your device

Your list, the due date, what you've bought or been given, the hospital bag and your settings stay on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android keeps a copy in your Google account so you can move everything to a new phone. The developer can't access that backup.

Sharing the list (optional)

Sharing is part of Lunido Pro and works only between Android phones. To share or join a list, each person signs in with Google. Lunido then uses two Google Firebase services. Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address, Google profile picture link and a user ID, and sees your IP address to protect sign-in from abuse. Cloud Firestore stores the shared plan: the baby's nickname, the due date, climate and region, item names, quantities, statuses, prices, the budget, gifts with the names of who gave them, notes, and whether the plan's creator has Pro.

Only the people in a plan can read or edit it. Invite codes have 8 characters and expire after 7 days. The data travels encrypted, and Google processes it for us under the Firebase terms. It's used only to keep the list in sync between your phones: never for ads or profiling, and it isn't shared with anyone else.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android. Nothing is sent to us.

Your region

You choose your region yourself. The app never asks for your location.

Deleting your data

Shared data stays in Firebase only while the plan exists: it's deleted when the plan's creator stops sharing, uses Delete everything or deletes their account. Settings › Share the list › Delete account deletes the plans you created, takes you out of the ones you joined and then deletes your account. Settings › Delete everything removes the list from your phone. Can't use the app? See Delete your account.

Purchases

Lunido Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

Lunido is made for adults preparing for a baby. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new text and date.

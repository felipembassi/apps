Delete your account

Updated October 27, 2026.

You only have a Lunido account if you signed in with Google to share your list on Android. Without one, everything is on your phone: Settings › Delete everything, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

In the app

Open Settings › Share the list, tap Delete account (it shows while you're signed in) and confirm.

What is deleted

Your Lunido account: the name, email address, profile picture link and user ID in Firebase Authentication. The shared plans you created: the plan, all its items and gifts, and its invite codes; the people you shared with go back to their own list. Plans you joined: you're removed from them.

What is kept

The list on your phone stays until you delete it there. A plan you joined belongs to the person who created it, so what you added to it stays until they delete it. Google Play keeps its own record of your Pro purchase. We keep no other copy; Google then clears the data from Firebase's backups over time.

Without the app

Lost your phone, or the app couldn't finish? Email felipe.mbassi@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with and ask us to delete your Lunido account. We'll delete it, with the shared plans you created, within 30 days and reply when it's done.

Primilu

Privacy policy

Updated October 27, 2026.

Primilu doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no account, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your baby's name and birth date, freezer batches, logged meals, allergen checks, plans, shopping list, your own foods and notes, and your settings stay on your iPhone. A note about a reaction stays on this iPhone only and never syncs. If iCloud is on, it syncs through Apple in your private iCloud database, which the developer can't access.

Sharing with your partner

If you share with your partner (Pro), Apple's iCloud sharing sends the shared data to the people you invite. We never see it.

Reminders

Thaw, stock and use-by reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. Nothing is sent to us.

Health information

Primilu is an organizer with references from official health bodies. It doesn't ask for symptoms, weight or health records and doesn't use HealthKit.

Purchases

Primilu Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new text and date.

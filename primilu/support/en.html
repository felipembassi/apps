Common questions

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

My data doesn't show up on my other device.

Check that both devices use the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for Primilu in the iOS Settings app (your name › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

Is this medical or nutrition advice?

No. Primilu organizes your baby's first foods with references from official health bodies in your country, each with its source. It doesn't replace your pediatrician or nutritionist; suggested plates and amounts are starting points.

I found something wrong in the content.

In Settings, tap Suggest a correction and tell us the food or rule and your country. We fix it in the next update.

Where do storage times come from?

From your country's official guidance when it gives a number (for Brazil, the Ministry of Health's Guia Alimentar, p. 169). When it doesn't, Primilu uses a conservative default marked “app default”. You can change the date of any batch.

Common questions

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

In the app, go to Settings › Clockable Pro and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

My data doesn't show up on my other device.

Check that both devices use the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for Clockable in the iOS Settings app (your name › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

How do I export my hours?

On the Summary screen, tap Export CSV (free) or Export PDF (Pro). Each one opens the share sheet.

Is this an official time clock?

No. Clockable is a personal record of your own hours. For payroll, your employer's records are the official ones.

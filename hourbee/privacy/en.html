Hourbee

Privacy policy

Updated October 27, 2026.

Hourbee doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no account, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your jobs, shifts, breaks, pay rates and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad. If iCloud is on, it syncs through Apple in your private iCloud database, which the developer can't access.

Exports

CSV and PDF files are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

Purchases

Hourbee Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new text and date.

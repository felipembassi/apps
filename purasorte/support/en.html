Common questions

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

My data doesn't show up on my other device.

Check that both devices use the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for Pura Sorte in the iOS Settings app (your name › iCloud). Syncing can take a few minutes.

Are the numbers really random?

Yes. They come from the system's cryptographic random generator. In the Proof tab you can run up to 1 million draws and see that no number is favored.

Does the app improve my odds?

No. No app can: every combination is equally likely. Pura Sorte doesn't sell tickets and isn't affiliated with any lottery operator.
